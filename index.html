Our Purpose

We're not teaching women how to get rich. We're teaching women how to build freedom.

Questions

Before You Hit the Trail

Do I need trading experience to join?

No. The Scout elevation assumes zero experience — it starts with what futures are, how markets work, and the language of trading.

How much does membership cost?

Membership is $97/month for the Scout, Tracker, and Pathfinder elevations. When you reach Trailblazer, your rate drops to $67/month. Guide is an optional hourly one-on-one coaching program available after completing all four elevations.

What are the five elevations?

Scout (learning the basics), Tracker (building consistency), Pathfinder (trading independently), Trailblazer (consistent profitability), and Guide (helping others succeed — the optional mentor track).

What is the Guide program?

Guide is hourly one-on-one coaching with the founder, offered once all four elevations are complete and the candidate is successfully and consistently earning a living in the market. It is not required — it's the path for members who want to mentor with Wild Women Trading Company.

When are the live sessions?

Morning Campfire runs twice per week for members at Pathfinder level and above — market prep, key levels, and a game plan from experienced mentors.
